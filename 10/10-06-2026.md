- The P(F|A) in french is 1/2
- The P(C|A) in chem is 2/3

If theres an equal chance she takes either course. Whats the Prob she gets an A
```
P(F|A) = 1/2
P(A) = 1/2 + 2/3

1/2 / (1/2 + 2/3)

P(F) = 1/2
P(C) = 1/2


(1/2 + 2/3)

P(A) = P(F) P(A|F) + P(C) P(A|C)

P(A) = 1/2 * 1/2 + 1/2 * 2/3
A = AF U AC

P(A|F) = P(AF) / P(F)
P(F) * P(A/F) = P(AF)
P(AF) = 1/2 * 1/2
P(AC) = 1/2 * 2/3

so P(A) = P(AF) + P(AC)
P(A) = (1/2 * 1/2) + (1/2 * 2/3)
```
