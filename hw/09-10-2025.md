16. How many 5-card poker hands are there?

    How many ways to pick 5 cards from 52 cards?

21. From a group of 8 women and 6 men, a committee consisting of 3 men and 3 women is to be formed. How many different committees are possible if:

    a. 2 of the men refuse to serve together?

    - 8 women
    - 6 men

    **ANS: (8 choose 3) * [(6 choose 3) - (6 - 2 choose 3 - 2)]**

    b. 2 of the women refuse to serve together?

    **ANS: [(8 choose 3) - (8 - 2 choose 3 - 2)] * (6 choose 3)**

    c. 1 man and 1 woman refuse to serve together?

    **ANS: [(8 choose 3) * (6 choose 3)] - [(8 - 1 choose 3 - 1) * (6 - 1 choose 3 - 1)]**
