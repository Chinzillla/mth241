MTH 241 · Exam preparation

Mixed mock exam

20 unlabeled questions · 100 practice points

Conditions: no notes, formula sheet, calculator, or answer key.

Answer style: leave answers as exact combinations, factorial ratios, products, sums, and fractions. Explain your model and what each factor or event means. Define notation you introduce.

Before every solution: write a brief method statement and the feature of the question that led you to it. There are no topic headings to supply that choice.

Suggested use: two sessions of 10 questions. As a practice option, allow about 60–75 minutes per session, then adjust based on your pace. This does not predict the real exam’s duration or format.

Each question is worth 5 self-study points: 2 for method/model, 2 for correct setup and conclusion, and 1 for explanation. The two sessions are 50 points each.

All three chapters are represented at your request. Later Chapter 3 and synthesis questions may exceed the eventual announced exam cutoff.
