# MTH 241 exam preparation

Prepared September 29, 2026. Ross, 10th edition, Chapters 1–3.

## Start here

1. Open ../output/pdf/grouped-practice.pdf (21 pages): 80 grouped problems plus 16 recognition drills, a decision guide, section coverage map, and two-week study sequence.
2. Attempt ../output/pdf/mixed-mock-exam.pdf (11 pages): 20 questions without method labels, split into two practice sessions.
3. Check ../output/pdf/worked-solutions.pdf (26 pages): worked symbolic solutions and 12 corrections/conventions from the notes.

Question IDs: A1–P5 refer to grouped problems; R1–R16 are recognition drills; X1–X20 are mixed exam questions. The mixed exam uses X to avoid confusion with Bayes group M.

No calculator or formula sheet during mock attempts. The guide is a study aid only. Exact combinatorial expressions, fractions, and sums are intentional.

All of Chapter 3 is included at your request; the actual exam cutoff within Chapter 3 remains unconfirmed. The class notes give October 15, 2026 as the exam date. The practice timing and point rubric are suggestions, not the instructor's format.

The HTML files are self-contained, browser-readable versions using native mathematical notation and local fonts. The JSON stores the questions and answers for future revisions. The grouped-practice.tex source was preserved, but the app's LaTeX compiler could not initialize in this environment; use the verified PDFs or HTML versions.

Validation: 39 independent mathematical checks, complete question/solution ID checks, and rendered-page visual review. Original course materials were not edited.
