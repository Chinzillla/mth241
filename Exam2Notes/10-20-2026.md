Example: A lab blood test is 95% effective in detecting a disease when person has it. However the test has a fals positive 1% of the time to people who dont have it. If .5% of the population has the disease, what is the probability somone has the disease given test is positive
```
P(A|B) = P(AB)/P(B)
P(A) = P(AB) + P(A-B)

E = test detects a disease
D = have disease

P(D) = .5% = .005 
P(D-) = .995
P(E|D) = 95% = .95
P(E|D-) = .01

P(ED) = P(E|D) * P(D)
P(ED) = .95 * .005 = 0.00475

P(ED-) = P(E|D-) * P(D-)
P(ED) = .01 * .995 = .00995

P(E|D-) = P(ED-) * P(D-)
P(D|E) = P(DE)/P(E)
P(DE) = P(ED)

0.0147 = P(E)

so P(D|E) = P(DE)/P(E) = 0.0475/0.0147

False positive is of .5% that have the disease
```

Example: which is concerned with an insurance company that believes that people can be divided into two distinct classes: those who are accident prone and those who are not. During any given year, an accident-prone person will have an accident with probability .4, whereas the corresponding figure for a person who is not prone to accidents is .2. 30% of people are accident prone. What is the probability a person will have an accident next year
```
P(A) = Accident Prone =.3
P(A-) = Not accident Prone = .7

P(A|Y) = .4
P(A|Y-) = .2

P(Y) = Accident next year

P(Y|A) = P(YA)/P(A)

P(A|Y) = P(AY)/P(Y)
Need to find P(Y)?

P(Y) = P(YA) + P(YA-)

P(YA) = P(A|Y) * P(A)
P(YA) = .4 * .3 = 0.12
P(YA-) = .2 * .7 = 0.14
P(Y) = 0.26

26% chance the probability someone will have an accident next year
```
